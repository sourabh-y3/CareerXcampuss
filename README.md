CAREERXCAMPASS
AI-Powered Career & Education Guidance Platform
📌 Overview

CAREERXCAMPASS is an AI-powered, one-stop career and education guidance platform that helps students make informed decisions after Class 10 and Class 12. It provides aptitude-based recommendations, personalized career roadmaps, and transparent comparison of government and private colleges—ensuring clarity, accessibility, and equal opportunity for all learners.

🎯 Problem Statement

Students often lack structured guidance on stream selection, college choices, and career outcomes. Decisions are influenced by peer pressure, parental expectations, or incomplete information, leading to wrong choices, dropouts, and financial strain. Existing solutions are fragmented and fail to provide end-to-end career navigation.

💡 Solution

CAREERXCAMPASS acts like a “Google Maps for Careers”, guiding students from school to career through AI-driven insights, visual roadmaps, and real-time support. It integrates college data, career paths, timelines, and skills into a single intelligent platform.

🚀 Key Features

Aptitude & interest-based career recommendations

Personalized career roadmaps

Government & private college directory

Side-by-side college comparison (fees, cut-offs, placements)

Admission, exam & scholarship alerts

Multilingual AI chatbot

Skill & learning resource recommendations

Offline-ready Progressive Web App (PWA)

🧱 Architecture Overview

CAREERXCAMPASS follows a clean, scalable architecture using the MERN stack integrated with Google Cloud and AI services. The system is divided into presentation, backend, AI, data, and cloud layers, ensuring modularity, security, and scalability.
